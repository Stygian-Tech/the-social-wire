# WGI Reference Review — October 4, 2026

Sports reviewed catalog v9 added Indoor Marching Arts; catalog v10 presents these existing identities under Marching Arts → WGI → Color Guard, Percussion, and Winds without changing their opaque IDs. The August 2026 [WGI Policy Manual](https://www.wgi.org/policymanual/) establishes 23 classes: eight Color Guard classes including Scholastic and Independent Regional A; nine Percussion classes including Scholastic Concert A/Open/World; six Winds classes. Percussion Marching and Concert ensembles have separate identities.

The reviewed ensemble snapshot uses the official [2026 finalist reviews](https://www.wgi.org/26finalistreview/), covering 259 ensembles: 94 Guard, 119 Percussion, and 46 Winds. These are 2026 finalists, not an exhaustive current registration directory. Class membership is labelled 2026; ensemble identity keys omit competitive class, while discipline and ensemble format remain distinct. Two source entries are excluded pending review: the Independent Open Guard “Blessed Sacrament” entry duplicates the Independent World name without a verifiable distinct ensemble identifier; the Winds Open entry “Evidece Winds from William Carey University” needs its apparent spelling error resolved against an independent primary identity source.

Source pages are `https://www.wgi.org/2026-{category}-class-finalist-review/`, for categories `sa`, `so`, `sw`, `ia`, `io`, `iw`, `percussion-concert`, `psa`, `pso`, `psw`, `pia`, `pio`, `piw`, `winds-a`, `winds-open`, and `winds-world`. Only factual names and classifications were reviewed; show descriptions, scores, photographs, and artwork are not imported.

News resolution requires indoor performance/discipline context. WGI initials, school names, Paramount, Matrix, Guard, and Winds alone are insufficient. Scholastic programs are separate across Guard, Percussion, and Winds; Concert and Marching percussion are distinct. Longer ensemble names prevent Infinity 2/3, Cap City 2, STRYKE Wynds 2, and similar groups from resolving as their parent ensemble. Local winter guard reporting may resolve Indoor Marching Arts without being assigned WGI membership.

No public event-data API or schedule/results reuse permission has been established in this review. WGI has no TheSportsDB mapping and is not enabled for events, scores, or standings. This work supplies a news reference catalog with generic icons; it does not authorize copying WGI media or scraping result tables. A separately reviewed adapter and rights evidence are required before activating WGI event data.
