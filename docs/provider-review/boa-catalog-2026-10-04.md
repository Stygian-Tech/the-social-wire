# Bands of America Reference Review — October 4, 2026

Sports catalog v10 adds a common Marching Arts presentation and selectable broad interest, with DCI, WGI, and BOA beneath it. Existing DCI and WGI opaque IDs, sport IDs, follows, and mutes remain valid. Contextual news in each activity also resolves the new Marching Arts umbrella, so its matching-only feed and broad follow/mute work across all three circuits.

The official [2026 Grand National Championships page](https://marching.musicforall.org/grand-national-championships/) reports a participant list last updated October 4, 2026. The reviewed catalog includes its 98 competing high-school bands; exhibition university bands are excluded. State-qualified identities keep similarly named schools separate. Bands receive a `2026 Grand Nationals Participant` label; this is a reviewed event-participation snapshot, not an exhaustive BOA membership directory.

BOA's [registration information](https://marching.musicforall.org/registrationinfo/) and [adjudication handbook](https://marching.musicforall.org/adjudication/) establish Classes A, AA, AAA, and AAAA by school enrollment. Four matching-only class feeds are available. The official Grand Nationals FAQ explains that classifications are withheld before and during events; the catalog does not guess class membership or expose inferred school enrollment. Class evidence must be explicit in an article before its class feed matches.

Reviewed news identities include BOA and Grand National Championships. Bare BOA financial/business mentions, a boa snake, school basketball, or horse racing's Grand National do not resolve to BOA. Bands require marching-band context or the named BOA organization; WGI school ensembles remain separate from the same school's outdoor marching band.

No documented public results API or permission to copy event tables was established. BOA has no TheSportsDB mapping, results, schedules, standings, or artwork activation. An independently reviewed adapter and rights evidence are required for event data. Only factual reference identities and generic icons are included.
